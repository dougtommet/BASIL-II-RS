Notes: Kappa is our participant-weighted extension of Conger's generalized kappa for the fixed panel of 7 reviewers. Severity uses quadratic agreement weights on the 0-10 scale; presence gives credit only to identical ratings. Exact agreement, agreement within 2 points, and mean absolute difference are calculated within participant, then averaged using entropy-balanced participant weights. Entropy balancing targets mean reference-standard delirium severity and site proportions in the full cohort (n = 488). Participant and rating counts are observed counts, not sums of weights. Parentheses contain 95% percentile confidence intervals from 2000 participant-bootstrap samples. Participant weights remain fixed during resampling; intervals omit weight-estimation uncertainty. CI, confidence interval. Strata retain the overall entropy weights; no separate dementia target is imposed. Estimates describe agreement within each dementia group. Category distributions influence kappa; differences do not by themselves establish differences in reviewer performance, and similar estimates do not establish equivalent agreement.
